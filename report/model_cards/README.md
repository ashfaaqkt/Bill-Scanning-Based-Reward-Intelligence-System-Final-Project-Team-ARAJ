## Reading these honestly

Three things are stated in the cards and worth repeating up front, because they
are the questions a viva panel will ask:

1. **The anomaly detector's 13.2% false-positive rate is *set*, not discovered.**
   It follows directly from the `contamination` parameter. Its recall is measured
   against *injected synthetic* outliers because no labelled real anomalies
   exist. Neither number means anything alone.

2. **The fraud CNN's headline 0.805 includes synthetic tampering.** 77% of the
   tampered class was generated by our own script. **0.864, measured on the 94
   real photographs, is the honest estimate of real-world performance.**

3. **The recommender uses a hybrid model.** It blends content-based category interests with an SVD collaborative filtering model trained on interaction logs, achieving a validated NDCG@5 of **0.7984**.

## Not machine learning

For completeness, since the pipeline mixes them: **perceptual-hash duplicate
detection** (`fraud.py:check_phash_duplicate`) is a deterministic algorithm, not
a learned model, and the **OCR pipeline** is a prompted Gemini call plus
classical CV checks (Laplacian blur, density anomaly).
